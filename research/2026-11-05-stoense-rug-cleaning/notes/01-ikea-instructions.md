# IKEA instructions: what they say and what they mean

## Product identity

- IKEA Denmark, **STOENSE tæppe, kort luv, råhvid, 130 cm**, article **704.268.01**.
  URL: https://www.ikea.com/dk/da/p/stoense-taeppe-kort-luv-rahvid-70426801/
- Materials: **Slitageside: 100% polypropylen**; **Bagsidebelægning: 100 % Syntetisk gummi**.
- Care text (Danish, verbatim):
  - “Kan ikke vaskes.”
  - “Tåler ikke klorblegning.”
  - “Må ikke tørres i tørretumbler.”
  - “Må ikke stryges.”
  - “Tåler ikke kemisk rens.”
  - “Støvsug tæppet og vend det ofte.”
  - “Tørre pletter fjernes straks ved at skrabe forsigtigt mod midten af pletten.”
  - “Gnid ikke på våde pletter. Sug væsken op med køkkenrulle, fugt med en klud og et

  - “Professionel tæpperens efter behov.”
  - “Brug altid det almindelige støvsugerhoved til gulve, når du støvsuger tæppet, aldrig

- The linked care sheet is a one-page PDF:
  https://www.ikea.com/dk/da/manuals/stoense-taeppe-kort-luv-rahvid__AA-2132679-2-2.pdf
  It could not be parsed in this environment (see audit); the product page carries the same

## IKEA itself glosses “do not wash” as “do not machine wash”

IKEA’s care FAQ says, under general rug care:

> “Do not machine wash.”
> “Do not bleach.”
> “Do not tumble dry.”
> “Do not iron.”
> “Do not dry clean.”

URL: https://www.ikea.com/in/en/customer-service/knowledge/articles/df48g043-0030-4134-850d-edb8e6f40c17.html

This is the key interpretive link: the product page’s “Do not wash” is not a prohibition on
ever getting the rug damp. IKEA immediately tells you to use a **damp cloth** and a **mild
detergent** on spots. The prohibition is against machine washing / full immersion. “Do not
dry clean” is a separate prohibition on solvent dry cleaning. “Professional carpet cleaning”
is therefore the water-based carpet-cleaning trade, not the dry-cleaning trade.

## Corroborating IKEA care guidance

IKEA’s rug guide (Canada) says:

> “It’s generally recommended that you should only wash a rug (if that is an option) when the
> dirt can’t be removed by vacuuming.”
> “As with all rugs, if a Persian rug becomes very dirty or needs to be repaired, we recommend
> that you use a professional rug cleaner. Special equipment is needed to clean this type of
> rug properly, to not damage the rug.”

URL: https://www.ikea.com/ca/en/files/pdf/eb/ea/ebeafd3a/rugs-home-furnishing-guide-fy23-l1-en.pdf

The guide is about Persian rugs, not STOENSE, but it confirms IKEA’s general position:
vacuum first; wash only when necessary; professional cleaning is for the cases where special
equipment is needed. For STOENSE, the “special equipment” issue is the rubber backing and
controlled moisture recovery, not the polypropylene face fibre.

## Interpretation

1. **Do not machine wash / do not immerse.** The rubber backing is the limiting component.
2. **Do not dry clean.** Solvent cleaning is explicitly excluded; “professional” cannot mean
   “chemical dry cleaning”.
3. **Spot cleaning with a damp cloth is explicitly allowed.** Water is not forbidden; excess
   water and poor drying are.
4. **Professional carpet cleaning is an allowed escalation**, presumably hot-water extraction
   or another controlled carpet-cleaning method, not a washing machine and not a dry-cleaning
   plant.
5. **The instruction is generic and conservative.** IKEA does not define “professional
   carpet cleaning” or name the synthetic rubber polymer. The exact safe moisture limit is
   therefore an inference from the material class, not a manufacturer specification.
