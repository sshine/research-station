# Denmark: rental vs professional cost for a 130 cm rug

## Rug area

Diameter 130 cm → radius 0.65 m.
Area = π × 0.65² = **1.327 m²**, rounded to **1.33 m²**.

## Interpretation

- For this small rug, **renting a machine is not clearly cheaper than hiring a professional**
  once you add the cleaning fluid, travel, deposit, and your time. A professional clean is
  roughly **250–725 DKK**; a rental is roughly **316–415 DKK** plus time.
- If you already own a wet/dry vacuum, DIY is much cheaper: a bottle of carpet cleaner is
  ~50–140 DKK, and you may already have suitable mild detergent.
- If you have several rugs or expect to clean annually, a rental or a small extractor makes
  more sense.
- The professional price is not only the cleaning; it includes method selection, controlled
  extraction, drying, and liability. For a 1.33 m² rug, that can be good value.
- Prices are a **5 November 2026 snapshot** and vary by store, distance, minimum charge, and
  whether pickup/delivery is included. Always get a written quote with the method.

## What to compare in a quote

Ask each provider:

1. Is the price per m² or a minimum visit fee?
2. Is pickup/delivery included?
3. What method (low-moisture HWE, encapsulation, full immersion)?
4. Do you clean rubber-backed rugs, and how do you protect the backing?
5. What is the drying time and guarantee?
6. Is the rug insured while in your care?
