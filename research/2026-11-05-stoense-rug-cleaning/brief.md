# Research brief

- **Original request:** Investigate how to clean an IKEA **STOENSE** rug, 130 cm diameter,
  “kort luv” (low pile): 100 % polypropylene wear surface, 100 % synthetic rubber backing.
  IKEA says “Kan ikke vaskes” / “Do not wash”, “Tåler ikke kemisk rens” / “Do not dry clean”,
  and “Professionel tæpperens efter behov” / “Professional carpet cleaning when needed”.
  The question: **how does a private person apply “professional” cleaning without highly
  specialised equipment?**
- **Sharpened question:** What does IKEA’s “professional carpet cleaning” mean for this
  specific rug, which parts of the professional process can a private person reproduce with
  household or rented equipment, and when is it cheaper/safer to hire a professional in
  Denmark?

## Scope

1. **Interpret the manufacturer instruction.** Separate “do not wash” (machine washing /
   full immersion) from “do not dry clean” (solvent cleaning) and “professional carpet
   cleaning when needed” (water-based deep cleaning by the carpet-cleaning trade).
2. **Establish the material constraints.** Polypropylene face fibre: hydrophobic,
   oleophilic, solution-dyed, low melting/softening point, poor crush recovery. Synthetic
   rubber backing: vulnerable to prolonged moisture, heat, and aggressive extraction.
3. **Establish what “professional” actually consists of.** The IICRC S100 / CRI process:
   pre-inspection, pre-vacuum, spot treatment, pre-spray, dwell, agitation, extraction/rinse,
   controlled drying, post-inspection. Distinguish hot-water extraction from true steam,
   dry cleaning, encapsulation, bonnet, and dry compound.
4. **Translate to a private-person protocol.** Three tiers: spot cleaning with no equipment;
   whole-rug extraction with a wet/dry shop vacuum; rented carpet extractor. Include
   chemistry, dilution, agitation, extraction, rinsing, and drying.
5. **Danish practicalities.** Rental prices/availability (Silvan, Bauhaus, XL Byg) and
   professional rug-cleaning prices per m², including pickup/delivery and minimum charges,
   for a 1.33 m² rug.
6. **Record disagreements and uncertainty.** Generic polypropylene advice often says “hose
   it down” or “steam clean”; that advice is about the face fibre and can conflict with
   IKEA’s backing-specific instruction. The specific rubber polymer is not disclosed.

## Out of scope

- Removing permanent dye, bleach, burn, or heat-fused damage.
- Treating mould or sewage contamination; those are restoration jobs, not maintenance.
- Recommending a specific brand of cleaning chemical as the only correct choice.
- Testing the actual rug; all conclusions are based on published instructions and material
  data, not a laboratory test of this specimen.
