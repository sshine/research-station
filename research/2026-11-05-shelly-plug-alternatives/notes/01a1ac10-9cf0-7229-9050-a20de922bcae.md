Compact Wi-Fi smart plug for remote control of electrical devices and energy consumption monitoring. Enables automatic switching of devices on and off through schedules and automations. Matter support, maximum load 2500 W.

**Shelly Plug S Gen3 is a compact and versatile Wi-Fi smart plug that allows ordinary electrical devices to be remotely controlled, scheduled, and automated without any electrical installation. The smart plug makes it easy to control lamps, heaters, and other household electrical devices directly from a smartphone while monitoring their energy consumption in real time. Supporting a maximum load of 2500 W, the smart plug connects directly to your home Wi-Fi network without requiring a separate hub. Thanks to Matter compatibility, it can also be integrated into popular smart home systems such as Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa.**

Shelly Plug S Gen3 is easy to set up: simply plug it into a standard wall socket and connect the electrical device you want to control. Once connected, the device can be managed through the Shelly Smart Control app, both at home and remotely. The built-in energy meter allows you to monitor, for example, a heater's electricity consumption in real time and review its energy usage history over longer periods. Versatile scheduling and automation features make it possible to set seasonal lights to turn on automatically at sunset and switch off during the night. Thanks to its compact design, the smart plug fits into almost any household socket.

The multicolour LED indicator surrounding the socket displays the device's operating status and can indicate the connected appliance's power consumption using different colours. The indicator's brightness and colour can be adjusted, while night mode allows the light to be dimmed, for example, in a bedroom. Shelly Plug S Gen3 can also function as a Wi-Fi range extender and Bluetooth gateway for compatible Shelly devices. Additionally, the smart plug can be connected to other Shelly devices, allowing, for example, a motion sensor to automatically turn on a connected lamp when movement is detected. **Three-year (36-month) warranty!**
