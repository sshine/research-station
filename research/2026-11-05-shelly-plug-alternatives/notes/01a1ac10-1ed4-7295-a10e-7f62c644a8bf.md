# Comparison of Shelly Plug S Gen3, Shelly Plug PM Gen3, Shelly Plug M Gen3, Shelly Outdoor Plug S Gen3

Modified on Wed, 11 Feb at 10:32 AM

The Shelly Gen3 plug family includes four models, each designed for different needs and scenarios.

### **Shelly Plug S MTR Gen3**

**Link:** [https://kb.shelly.cloud/knowledge-base/shelly-plug-s-mtr-gen3](https://kb.shelly.cloud/knowledge-base/shelly-plug-s-mtr-gen3?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Shelly Plug S MTR Gen3 is a **Wi-Fi smart plug with Matter support**, designed for reliable remote control and power monitoring. It offers a multicolor LED indicator, scripting capabilities, and can act as a Wi-Fi range extender and BLE gateway.

**Key Features**

* Matter certified
* Wi-Fi smart plug with power metering
* Multicolor LED indication
* Scripting support
* Wi-Fi range extender & BLE gateway
* Schedules, scenes, and local actions
* Compatible with cloud and local control

**Use Cases**

* Remote control and energy tracking for household devices

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **Shelly Plug PM Gen3**

**Link:** [https://kb.shelly.cloud/knowledge-base/shelly-plug-pm-gen3](https://kb.shelly.cloud/knowledge-base/shelly-plug-pm-gen3?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Shelly Plug PM Gen3 is a **Wi-Fi smart plug with power measurement capabilities only** for monitoring and analyzing energy consumption. It helps optimize energy use and prevents unnecessary waste while offering scripting and broad compatibility.

**Key Features**

* Multicolor LED indicator
* Power metering and energy monitoring only
* Scripting support
* Wi-Fi range extender & BLE gateway
* Compatible with cloud and local control

**Use Cases**

* Appliances where energy tracking is needed

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **Shelly Plug M Gen3**

Shelly Plug M Gen3 is a **compact Wi-Fi smart plug** focused on basic smart control with Matter support It can control devices remotely and supports LED status indication and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth setup.

**Key Features**

* **Smart on/off control**
* Internal power metering and voltage/current measurement
* LED status indication
* Bluetooth and Wi-Fi connectivity
* Scripting support

**Use Cases**

* Everyday devices where simple smart switch control and basic meter data is needed

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **Shelly Outdoor Plug S Gen3**

**Link:** [https://www.shelly.com/products/shelly-outdoor-plug-s-gen3](https://www.shelly.com/products/shelly-outdoor-plug-s-gen3?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The Outdoor Plug S Gen3 is designed specifically for **outdoor environments** with **IP44 protection** against splashes, making it suitable for terraces, gardens, and other outdoor equipment. It offers power metering, multicolor LED indication, and support for smart home integrations including Matter.

**Key Features**

* Weather-resistant (IP44 protection)
* Power metering and energy monitoring
* Multicolor LED indicator
* Wi-Fi range extender & BLE gateway
* Scripting, schedules, and scenes

**Use Cases**

* Outdoor lights and appliances
* Energy-efficient outdoor automation
