## Why the gap exists

The `just research` pipeline stored only 29 sources for this question. Those
entries are weighted toward search-result pages and market reports; the final
human synthesis then went to manufacturer and retailer pages directly. The
pipeline ledger was not extended afterwards, so the report cites sources that the
ledger does not contain. The next run should either add those pages to
`sources.jsonl` or write a `notes/references.md` file at synthesis time.
