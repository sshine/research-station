Smart plugs that do what the Shelly Plug S Gen3 did

The S Gen3 is discontinued, but it is still on Danish shelves. Here is the official replacement, the non-Shelly alternatives that match its local-control and power-monitoring job, and what to search for instead of “Shelly”.

Denmark Prices in DKK incl. VAT Local-first focus

The short answer

Unless you specifically need the smaller S Gen3 body, buy the M Gen3.
Default buy

Shelly Plug M Gen3

Official successor. 13 A / 3000 W, Matter, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, power metering, MQTT and a local web UI. Same approach as the S Gen3, higher load rating.

137 DKK 2-pack at Proshop · ~69 DKK/plug
Exact S Gen3

Residual stock only

avXperten has the white at 115 DKK in stock. Proshop has black at 116 DKK and white at 137 DKK on remote warehouse. Treat it as end-of-line stock.

Is “Shelly” becoming a generic keyword?

Short answer: not at Proshop. It is a brand, but also a compatibility label.

It is still a brand

A Proshop search for shelly plug returned 9 varer, all genuine Shelly. A shelly kompatibel search returned Shelly accessories, not a clone shelf.

Proshop `shelly plug` · Proshop `shelly kompatibel`

But it is used as a compatibility label

avXperten sells an “EcoFlow Stream Smart Plug — Shelly Kompatibel, 2500W” for 359 DKK. The product page lists brand as both Shelly and EcoFlow. It is an official EcoFlow × Shelly co-brand, not a counterfeit — and it only works with EcoFlow’s STREAM system.

What I would buy

Pick by the constraint that matters most to you.

I want the same thing, cheapest

Shelly Plug M Gen3 2-pack, 137 DKK at Proshop. About 69 DKK per plug.

I want the exact S Gen3

avXperten white 115 DKK (in stock) or Proshop black 116 DKK (remote warehouse).

I want no cloud

Eve Energy 268 DKK if 11 A is enough and you have a Thread border router; Nous A1T €16.67 if Tasmota/MQTT is fine.

I already have Zigbee

Sonoff S60ZBTPF 120 DKK (16 A) for value, or Aqara H2 EU 259 DKK for a polished dual-protocol plug.

I want cheap Matter

Osram SMART+ Matter 10 A, 90 DKK for lamps and small appliances. The 16 A outdoor version is 122 DKK.

I want outdoor

Shelly Outdoor Plug S Gen3 167 DKK for the Shelly ecosystem; Osram Matter Outdoor 122 DKK for cheap Matter/IP44.

I only want to measure

Shelly Plug PM Gen3, 143 DKK. No relay — it cannot switch the load.

I want multiple outlets

Proshop lists the Shelly Power Strip Gen4: four individually switched, individually metered outlets, 12 A per outlet / 16 A total.

Caveats

The two big ones: stock is a snapshot, and “Matter” does not guarantee metering.

Prices and stock move

This is a 5 November 2026 snapshot. Proshop uses “fjernlager” for remote warehouse stock, not “restlager”; expected own-stock dates were 06-11 and 10-11-2026 for the S Gen3 colours. Check the listing before ordering.

“Local” and “Matter” are not promises

A plug can pair locally while its energy history stays in the vendor app. Meross and some other Matter plugs have been app-only for power data in practice. Zigbee reporting also depends on the coordinator: Zigbee2MQTT exposed more entities than ZHA for the Sonoff S60ZBTPF.
