Smart plugs that do what the Shelly Plug S Gen3 did

The S Gen3 is discontinued, but it is still on Danish shelves. Here is the official replacement, the non-Shelly alternatives that match its local-control and power-monitoring job, and what to search for instead of “Shelly”.

Bottom line

  1. The Shelly Plug S Gen3 is officially discontinued. Shelly’s own product page says it is “no longer produced and sold by Shelly Group” and names the Shelly Plug M Gen3 as the recommended successor. The M Gen3 is the default buy: 13 A / 3000 W, Matter, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, power metering, MQTT and a local web UI — and it is cheaper than the old S Gen3. Shelly S Gen3 [1] · Shelly M Gen3 [2]
  2. You can still get the S Gen3 as residual stock. Proshop had the black at 116 DKK and the white at 137 DKK on “fjernlager” (remote warehouse, 2–5 days); avXperten had the white at 115 DKK in stock. Treat this as end-of-line stock, not a long-term supply. Proshop search [3] · avXperten [4]
  3. If you want another brand, the best all-round alternative is the Aqara Power Plug H2 EU (259 DKK, 16 A / 3680 W, Matter over Thread or Zigbee, energy monitoring). For privacy/no cloud, choose Eve Energy (268 DKK, Matter/Thread, no Eve cloud). For a cheap Zigbee plug, choose Sonoff S60ZBTPF (120 DKK, 16 A, needs a hub). For budget Matter, Osram SMART+ Matter 10 A is 90 DKK if 10 A is enough.
  4. “Shelly” has not become a generic word for smart plugs. A Proshop search for shelly plug returned 9 varer, all genuine Shelly. The alternatives appear when you search the category (smart stikkontakt, smart plug). “Shelly kompatibel” is used as a compatibility label, and at least one such product is an official EcoFlow × Shelly co-brand, not a clone.

1. What the Shelly Plug S Gen3 actually is

Shelly’s knowledge base also calls it the Shelly Plug S MTR Gen3 (model S3PL-00112EU); “MTR” is the Matter-capable variant name, not a separate product. Shelly KB [5]

Key specs from Shelly’s product page: 12 A / 2500 W resistive load, 44 × 44 × 70 mm, Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz + Bluetooth, Matter, power/energy metering, multicolor LED, scripting, webhooks, MQTT, local web interface, no hub required. Shelly S Gen3 [1]

Shelly’s own shop on 5 November 2026 showed the S Gen3 and PM Gen3 sold out, while the M Gen3 (108 DKK) and Outdoor (265 DKK) were in stock. That is consistent with the S Gen3 being phased out and the M Gen3 being the replacement.

3. Alternatives worth buying

Same brand (simplest path)

  • Shelly Plug M Gen3 — the official successor. 13 A / 3000 W, Matter, Wi-Fi/BT, metering, MQTT, local web UI, scripting. 108 DKK single from Shelly; 137 DKK for a 2-pack at Proshop (about 69 DKK each), 259 DKK for a 4-pack. It is 15 mm taller than the S Gen3 but otherwise the same idea, with a higher load rating. Shelly M Gen3 [2] · Proshop search [3]
  • Shelly Plug PM Gen3 — only if you want to measure an always-on appliance and never switch it. 16 A / 3680 W, no relay, no Matter. 143 DKK at Proshop. Shelly comparison [6]
  • Shelly Outdoor Plug S Gen3 — IP44, 12 A / 2500 W, Matter, metering. 167 DKK at Proshop.
  • Shelly Wave Plug S LR — Z-Wave 800 Long Range version if you already have a Z-Wave hub. 398 DKK from Shelly. Shelly smart plugs [6]

Specs and caveats:

  • Eve Energy is the privacy option: Matter over Thread, no Eve cloud, no registration. EU version is 11 A / 2500 W, so it is a lamp/office plug, not a heater plug. A Thread border router is required. Eve [18]
  • Aqara H2 EU is the strongest all-round non-Shelly choice: 16 A / 3680 W, energy monitoring, Matter over Thread or Zigbee 3.0. You need a Thread border router or an Aqara hub; it is not a Wi-Fi plug. Aqara [19]
  • Sonoff S60ZBTPF is the cheap high-load Zigbee option: 16 A / 4000 W, Zigbee 3.0, metering, repeater. It needs a Zigbee hub. Zigbee2MQTT exposes all entities; ZHA has had missing features in reviews, so check the current state before buying. CNX review [20]
  • THIRDREALITY E2 is also 16 A Zigbee with real-time metering, but it was out of stock at Proshop on the check date.
  • frient Mini 2 is well built but only 10 A continuous; it is not for a 16 A heater. frient [21]
  • Osram SMART+ Matter is the budget Matter route: 90 DKK for 10 A / 2300 W, 122 DKK for the 16 A / 3680 W IP44 outdoor version. Both are Matter over Wi-Fi, so no hub, but you need a Matter controller.
  • Meross Matter is a 16 A plug and cheap for a 2-pack, but its energy data is primarily in the Meross app. Several users report that Matter controllers get on/off but not power/energy. MSS315 specs [22] · Matter energy-monitoring caveat [23]
  • Bosch [+M] supports Matter/Thread and Zigbee and measures power, but it is expensive and Zigbee2MQTT users report missing power data. Buy it for the Bosch ecosystem, not as a cheap Shelly replacement. Bosch help [24] · power measurement [25]
  • Gosund EP2-Z is a cheap 10 A Zigbee plug; fine for lamps and small appliances, not for high-load heating.
  • Nous A1T is the closest thing to a “Shelly for tinkerers”: factory-installed Tasmota, MQTT, REST API, local web UI, no cloud, 10 A / 2400–2500 W, compact 46 × 46 × 72 mm. It is not sold at Proshop; the EU warehouse price was €16.67. Nous [26] · EU shop [27]

4. Is “Shelly” becoming a keyword for copy-cats?

Partly, but not in the way it first looks.

  • Shelly is still a brand. Proshop’s shelly plug search returned 9 genuine Shelly products and no third-party lookalikes. A shelly kompatibel search returned Shelly accessories, not a clone shelf. Proshop shelly plug [3] · Proshop shelly kompatibel [28]
  • “Shelly kompatibel” is a compatibility label. avXperten sells an “EcoFlow Stream Smart Plug — Shelly Kompatibel, 2500W” for 359 DKK. The product page lists brand as both Shelly and EcoFlow. EcoFlow itself calls it the “EcoFlow × Shelly Smart Plug” (€34.90, 12 A, 44 × 44 × 70 mm, 2500 W) and says it is only compatible with the EcoFlow STREAM system. This is a co-brand, not a counterfeit. avXperten [29] · EcoFlow [30]
  • Shelly has an official third-party program. “Powered by Shelly” devices contain a Shelly connectivity module and work with the Shelly app; the product itself is made by the partner. Shelly’s current examples are the Ogemray 25 A relay, Ecowitt WS90 weather station, FrankEver sprinkler controller and similar devices — no smart plugs in that collection on the check date. Program definition [31] · Collection [32]
  • Independent brands are not copies. Sonoff, Aqara, Eve, Meross, Osram, Bosch, Nous and Athom have their own apps, protocols and trade-offs. Reviewers call them “Shelly alternatives” because they match the feature set, not because they imitate the brand.
  • Counterfeits exist on marketplaces, so buy from a reputable retailer and check the model/box. There are public reports of lookalike Shelly PM Mini boxes on Amazon (example [33]), but I found no evidence of a widespread counterfeit Shelly-plug shelf in Danish retail. The more likely explanation for “a bunch of alternatives” is a broader search query or compatibility wording.

6. What I would buy

  • Simplest, best value: Shelly Plug M Gen3 2-pack, 137 DKK at Proshop. Same local API/MQTT/Matter approach as the S Gen3, higher 13 A rating, and cheaper per plug. If you only need one, 108 DKK from Shelly.
  • Exact S Gen3: avXperten white 115 DKK (in stock) or Proshop black 116 DKK (remote warehouse). Do not pay much more; it is discontinued.
  • No cloud / privacy: Eve Energy (268 DKK) if 11 A is enough and you have a Thread border router; Nous A1T (€16.67) if you are comfortable with Tasmota and MQTT.
  • Zigbee hub already installed: Sonoff S60ZBTPF (120 DKK, 16 A) for value, or Aqara H2 EU (259 DKK, 16 A, Matter/Thread + Zigbee) for a more polished dual-protocol plug.
  • Cheap Matter: Osram SMART+ Matter 10 A (90 DKK) for lamps/small appliances; the 16 A outdoor version is 122 DKK.
  • Outdoor: Shelly Outdoor Plug S Gen3 (167 DKK) if you want the Shelly ecosystem; Osram SMART+ Matter Outdoor (122 DKK) if you want cheap Matter/IP44.
  • Measure only, never switch: Shelly Plug PM Gen3 (143 DKK). Do not buy this expecting on/off control.

7. Caveats

  • Prices and stock are a 5 November 2026 snapshot. Proshop’s search results and expected stock dates change; “fjernlager” is not the same as “on the shelf”.
  • “Local control” is not binary. A plug can pair locally over Matter or Zigbee while its energy history stays in the vendor app. Check that the controller you use exposes the metering cluster, not just on/off.
  • Zigbee energy reporting depends on the coordinator. The Sonoff review found Zigbee2MQTT exposed more entities than ZHA; check current firmware/integration before relying on it.
  • Matter energy reporting is still uneven across brands. Eve exposes power/energy through Matter; Meross has been app-only in practice (Matter energy-monitoring caveat [23]). Do not assume that a “Matter” label means your controller will see the meter.
  • Shelly’s 2026 catalogue also shows a new Plug C Gen4 / Plug C PM Gen4 cord-end design, but it was not broadly listed in Danish retail at the time of this check. If you can wait, it may be the more space-saving Gen4 option. Matter-SmartHome [35]
  • I did not audit every Danish webshop or marketplace. The shortlist is based on Shelly’s official pages, Proshop/avXperten listings, and manufacturer documentation retrieved on the check date.
